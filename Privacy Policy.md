# Privacy Policy (PortGhost)

**Last updated:** 2026-03-17

PortGhost is a desktop application. This Privacy Policy explains how PortGhost handles information when you use it.

## Summary

- **No accounts / no logins.**
- **No tracking / no analytics / no telemetry.**
- **No third-party integrations.**
- **No API connections.**
- PortGhost is intended to run **locally on your device**, and we do not receive your data.

## Information We Collect

### Information collected by the app
**PortGhost does not collect, transmit, or store personal information on our servers** because it does not connect to any servers or third-party services.

### Information you provide voluntarily (outside the app)
If you contact us (for example, by opening a GitHub Issue or sending an email), you may voluntarily provide information such as:
- Your name/handle
- Email address (if you email us)
- The content of your message
- Diagnostic details you choose to include (e.g., screenshots or error messages)

If you post in a public forum (such as GitHub Issues), that content is public.

## How We Use Information

If you contact us, we use the information you provide only to:
- Respond to your request
- Troubleshoot problems you report
- Improve PortGhost (based on feedback and bug reports)

## Sharing

We do not sell or share personal information.

If you choose to post information in public places (e.g., GitHub Issues), that information is shared publicly by you.

## Data Retention

- The PortGhost application does not transmit your data to us, so we do not retain application data.
- Messages you send us (or public posts you create) may be retained as long as needed to respond and maintain project history. Public posts may remain available indefinitely unless you remove them via the platform.

## Security

Because PortGhost runs locally and does not send data to us, privacy risk is minimized. You are responsible for securing your own device and any files you choose to use with PortGhost.

## Children’s Privacy

PortGhost is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy, we will update the **Last updated** date above. If PortGhost’s behavior changes in a way that affects privacy (for example, adding network features), we will update this policy accordingly.
